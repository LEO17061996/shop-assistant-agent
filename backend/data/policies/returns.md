# Returns and refunds — Kestrel Home (demo store)

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery.

## Condition
Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Assembled furniture can be returned only if it is undamaged and has been disassembled for shipping.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Rugs that have been cut or custom-sized
- Personalized or made-to-order items
- Pillow inserts removed from their sealed packaging (hygiene)
- Clearance items marked "final sale"

## Return shipping cost
US: we email a free return label for orders over $99. For smaller orders, $9.95 is deducted from the refund.
US freight items (sofas, beds, headboards): a $99 pickup fee is deducted from the refund.
UK: the customer arranges and pays for return postage. We recommend a tracked service.

## Refund timing
Refunds are processed within 5 business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse. The money goes back to the original payment method; banks take another 3–10 business days to show it.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items
Report the problem within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and the packaging. We send a replacement or a full refund, and you pay no return shipping. Damage claims are handled by a human agent.

## Exchanges
We do not do direct exchanges. Return the item and place a new order.
