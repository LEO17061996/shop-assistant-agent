# Care guide — Kestrel Home (demo store)

## Solid wood
Dust with a soft, dry cloth. Keep away from direct sunlight, radiators and heaters. Use coasters under drinks. Wood is happiest at 35–55% room humidity; very dry air can cause small cracks.

## Wood veneer and engineered wood
Do not let water sit on the surface. Wipe with a slightly damp cloth, then dry immediately. Avoid harsh cleaners and furniture polish with silicone.

## Upholstery (fabric)
Vacuum weekly with a brush attachment. Blot spills right away; do not rub. Check the cleaning code on the label:
- W: water-based cleaner
- S: solvent-based cleaner only
- W/S: either
- X: vacuum only, no liquids

## Leather and faux leather
Wipe with a damp cloth. Condition genuine leather every 6–12 months. Keep leather at least 2 feet from heat sources to stop it drying and cracking. Faux leather does not need conditioner.

## Rugs
Vacuum regularly; turn off the beater bar for high-pile or shag rugs. Rotate the rug every 6 months so it wears evenly. A rug pad stops slipping and extends its life. Spot clean small spills; wool rugs should be professionally cleaned.

## Metal
Wipe with a dry cloth. Metal furniture used outdoors should be covered or stored in winter.
