# Shipping policy — Kestrel Home (demo store)

## Where we ship
We ship to the United States (all 50 states) and the United Kingdom (England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland). We do not ship to the EU or other countries yet.
Sofas and Beds & Headboards ship to the US only because they travel by freight. Every product lists the countries it ships to.
Alaska and Hawaii orders have a $49 surcharge on furniture items.

## Dispatch and delivery times
In-stock items are dispatched within 1–2 business days.
US standard delivery takes 3–7 business days after dispatch.
US freight items (sofas, beds, headboards) use white-glove delivery: 7–14 business days, and the carrier calls to schedule a delivery window.
UK delivery takes 7–14 business days after dispatch via Royal Mail or DHL.

## Shipping costs
US: free standard shipping on orders of $99 or more. Orders under $99 pay a flat $9.95.
US freight items: flat $149 per order, regardless of order value.
UK: flat $24.95 per order, free on orders of $250 or more.

## UK customs, duties and VAT
For UK orders with goods worth £135 or less, UK VAT (20%) is added at checkout. Nothing more is due on delivery.
For UK orders above £135, VAT and any import duty are collected by the carrier before delivery and are paid by the customer.
If a parcel is held at customs, it usually clears in 2–5 business days. Contact support with your order ID if it takes longer.

## Out-of-stock items
Items with zero stock cannot be ordered. Customers can sign up for a back-in-stock email on the product page. Restocks usually take 3–6 weeks.

## Changing or cancelling an order
You can change the delivery address or cancel within 2 hours of placing the order, as long as it has not been dispatched. After dispatch, the order follows the returns policy.
