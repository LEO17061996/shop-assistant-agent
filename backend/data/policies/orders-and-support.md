# Orders, payments and support — Kestrel Home (demo store)

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. Klarna (4 interest-free payments) is available for US orders only.

## Currency
All prices are in US dollars. UK cards are charged in USD and the bank applies its own conversion rate.

## Price changes and discount codes
We do not price match other stores. If the price of an item you bought drops within 7 days of purchase, we refund the difference once.
Only one discount code can be used per order, and codes cannot be added after the order is placed. Support staff and the shopping assistant cannot create discount codes.

## Order status
To check an order we need both the order ID (format KH-12345) and the email address used at checkout.

## Trade and bulk orders
Orders of more than 10 units of the same item go through our trade program. Email trade@kestrelhome.example with the item and quantity.

## Talking to a person
Human support is available Monday to Friday, 9am–6pm US Eastern Time, by email at support@kestrelhome.example or through a live chat handoff. We reply within 1 business day.
Complaints, refund disputes, damage claims and warranty claims are always handled by a person.

## Privacy and security
We never ask for full card numbers, passwords or one-time codes in chat.
