# Warranty — Kestrel Home (demo store)

## Coverage period
All products have a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects, starting from the delivery date.

## What is covered
- Structural defects in frames, joints and legs
- Faulty hardware (hinges, drawer slides, bolts supplied with the item)
- Electrical components of lamps and light fixtures

## What is not covered
- Normal wear and tear, including fabric pilling
- Fading from direct sunlight
- Damage from pets, misuse or incorrect assembly
- Indoor furniture used outdoors
- Commercial or rental use
- Light bulbs

## How to make a claim
Email support with your order ID and photos of the defect. We will repair the item, send a replacement part, or replace the product, at our discretion. Warranty claims are reviewed by a human agent.
